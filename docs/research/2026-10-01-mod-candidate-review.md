# No Man’s Land — organized candidate mod list

Updated 2026-10-01. Proposed member research and test preferences only; no candidate is approved or registered by this document. No mods have been installed or tested, no server configuration or addon dependency has changed, and Stage 5.3 has not started. NML is an open-world faction combat sandbox with no objectives, capture points, score, rounds or win condition. Third-party mods remain external Workshop dependencies.

**Selected** records the member's preference for a future test. **Retained** means an original suggestion kept for consideration, without a confirmed conflict requiring removal. These labels are research preferences, not changes to the repository's candidate lifecycle or lead approval. Neither status means the combined setup has passed a server test. Workshop links identify the package; the full dependency tree must be resolved before a test configuration is built. Console support is not a selection criterion.
